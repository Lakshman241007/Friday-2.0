EVALUATION DIMENSIONS:
1. Opening:
   - Evaluates introduction, establishment of call purpose, appropriate addressing of customer, and natural conversation entry.
   - Look for: Did employee introduce themselves? Was the purpose established clearly? Was there unnecessary friction?

2. Discovery:
   - Evaluates whether the employee sought to understand the customer's needs, requirements, pain points, and current situation.

   - If the call nature does not require discovery (e.g., pure inbound confirmation or brief administrative verification), mark applicable = false.

3. Explanation:
   - Evaluates how clearly the employee communicated the product, service, or solution.
   - Look for: Clarity, connecting features directly to customer's stated needs/pain points, avoiding jargon, clear communication.
   - Ground evaluation strictly in supplied conversation context. Do not invent external product facts.

   - CRITICAL RULE: If NO customer objections occurred during the call, you MUST set "applicable": false and "score": null. Do NOT assign a low score when no objections were raised.

5. Closing:
   - Evaluates how the conversation concluded.
   - Look for: Clear next step agreed upon, follow-up date/time established where relevant, addressing lingering questions, clear call-to-action.
   - Note: Do NOT assume every call must end in a closed deal; support or exploratory calls have different appropriate closings.

- Applicable dimensions receive an integer score between 0 and 100:
